# VoxelRay Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026

VoxelRay ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Zero Data Collection
- **No Personal Data Collected:** VoxelRay does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, crash telemetry, location data, or device identifiers.
- **100% On-Device Rendering:** All ray tracing, procedural voxel generation, Photo Mode image synthesis, and destruction physics run strictly on your iPhone using Apple Metal and SwiftUI.
- **On-Device Purchases:** In-App Purchases (`Architect Pro` and `Prism Material Pack`) are processed and verified exclusively through Apple's StoreKit 2 framework on your device. VoxelRay has no backend servers and does not receive your payment details or Apple Account identity.
- **No Tracking or Third-Party SDKs:** VoxelRay contains zero third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs (`NSPrivacyTracking = false`).
- **Photo Exports:** When you tap **Photo Mode**, images are rendered locally and shared only to destinations you explicitly choose in the iOS Share Sheet (`UIActivityViewController`).
