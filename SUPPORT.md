# VoxelRay Support

Welcome to **VoxelRay** — the ray-traced destructible voxel sandbox for iPhone.

## Controls
- **Move:** Drag the bottom-left virtual joystick to fly around the voxel world.
- **Look:** Drag anywhere on the right side of the 3D viewport to orbit your view.
- **Carve / Sculpt:** Tap directly on any voxel structure to blast a crater (using the **Blast** radius slider) or place iridescent voxels with the **Prism** brush.
- **Sun Angle:** Drag the **Sun** slider to shift the Nishita atmospheric sky lighting from low twilight to high noon.
- **Reset World:** Tap **Reset World** in the bottom-right corner to restore the active biome deterministically.
- **Photo Mode:** Tap **Photo Mode** in the top bar to converge 64 path-traced samples and export via the iOS Share Sheet.

## Troubleshooting Purchases
- **Restore Purchases:** Open the **Studio / Pro** sheet from the top bar and tap **Restore Purchases** to sync existing StoreKit 2 entitlements on your Apple Account.
- **Missing Hardware Features:** Metal 4 hardware acceleration structures and MetalFX Temporal Upscaling require physical iPhone hardware running iOS 26.0 or later.
